About the Game

  • Tactical fantasy TCG with Ley Fonts as your resource engine.
  • Five card types: Units, Runes, Relics, Strategies, Tactics.
  • Playable prototype factions: Nobles and Brutes. Tricksters and Outcasts are planned for later playtests.
  • Objective: Reduce the enemy Command Station to 0.
Playtime: 25–40 minutes • Learn: 15 minutes
Status: Command Siege prototype in active development.
Playtesting: Available on DuelTheory.

Play the Prototype

  1. Open Ashen Throne on DuelTheory to view the rules, cards, and Play option.
  2. Choose a local duel for two players on one device, or create a private AT match and share the join link with a friend.
  3. Pick your decks. In a private room, both players press Ready before the coin flip.

Rules and keyword references are available inside the duel. The prototype is still being tested; card interactions and the interface may change.

Found a problem? Tell us which cards were involved, what happened, and what you expected. Email designteam@ashenforgegames.com.
